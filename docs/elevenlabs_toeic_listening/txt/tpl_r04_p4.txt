You're listening to Morning Wave on 94.5 FM. Before the weather report, here's a community update for listeners in Brookside. This Saturday, April twelfth, the annual Riverfront Craft Market returns to Reed Park from ten A.M. to five P.M.
<break time="0.8s" />
More than seventy local potters, woodworkers, and textile artists will set up booths along the main promenade. Because Bridge Street will be closed to vehicle traffic all day, organizers recommend parking free of charge at Brookside High School and riding the complimentary shuttle bus, which runs every ten minutes.
<break time="0.8s" />
To see a map of vendor booths and live acoustic music times, visit brooksidecraft.org.
