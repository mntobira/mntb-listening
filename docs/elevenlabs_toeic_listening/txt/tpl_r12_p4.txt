Hi everyone! As part of Redwood Analytics' autumn wellness initiative, Human Resources is launching a four-week team walking challenge starting October first. Employees can form teams of four to six people across any department and log their daily steps through the StrideWell mobile app.
<break time="0.8s" />
Every team that averages at least eight thousand steps per person each day will be entered into a drawing for gift certificates to local sporting goods stores. Don't worry if you prefer cycling or swimming—the app includes an activity converter so non-walking workouts count too.
<break time="0.8s" />
If you'd like to participate but don't have a full team yet, sign up as an individual on the HR portal by this Friday, and we'll match you with colleagues looking for teammates. Let's get moving!
